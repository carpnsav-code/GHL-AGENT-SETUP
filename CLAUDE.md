## Video production

Social video work for Mint Concrete Polishing follows a fixed standard operating
procedure: **read `docs/VIDEO-SOP.md` before starting any video task.**

The short version — script first and approved before anything is generated, then
a motion graphic every 3–5 seconds, then client B-roll, then the ElevenLabs
voiceover, then Malloy graphics each with their own sound effect, then assembly.

**No captions on these videos, ever.**
